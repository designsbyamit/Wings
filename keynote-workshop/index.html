1 / ICE-BREAKER

The Career Detective game.

I will give you a mystery career. Your job: guess the person's real superpower.

Clue 01They changed roles several times.
Clue 02They worked across products, teams and industries.
Clue 03People kept asking them to solve the messy, ambiguous stuff.
Your move: In pairs, invent the person's superpower in 30 seconds. Then we reveal the trick: the superpower is usually hidden in the pattern, not the job title.

not a logo.

It is the expectation people carry about the value you create.

Point of viewWhat do you consistently believe?
ProofWhat have you repeatedly demonstrated?
PresenceWhat do people experience around you?

KEYNOTE / A VEDIC LENS

Four forces
shape a career.

01DharmaWhat feels meaningful? Where do you create contribution?
02ArthaWhat creates value, livelihood and opportunity?

KEYNOTE / LOOKING BACK

Don't ask only
“What did I do?”

Look for recurrence.

What kinds of problems keep finding you?

What do people repeatedly ask you to help with?

What work keeps showing up even when your title changes?

Look for energy.

When did you feel unusually alive?

Which projects made you lose track of time?

What are you still curious about after the applause ends?
