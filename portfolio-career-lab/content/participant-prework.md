# Participant Pre-work

Bring whatever you have. Do not clean it first.

Accepted inputs:
- resume
- LinkedIn export or profile text
- portfolio URL
- project PDFs
- case studies
- plain text
- audio reflection
- spreadsheets
- screenshots/images

The app will turn messy material into a common career dataset.

Your only job before the workshop:
1. upload what you have
2. review obvious extraction errors
3. explore your visualisations
4. paste one surprising visualisation into the shared Career Wall
5. arrive curious, not polished
